Soul Power World Tour

A concert poster for David Tao's 2026 tour, balancing a live-stage portrait with clear event typography.

01 / Design challenge

Give the live performance a strong visual focal point.

The design balances the atmosphere of a concert with the event details in one vertical composition. The performer, artist name, year, and tour title each need a clear place in the visual hierarchy.

02 / Visual approach

Let the stage portrait and tour title lead.

Deep blue stage lighting sets the mood and makes the performer the main focal point. White typography identifies David Tao and the year at the top, while the large tour title anchors the lower part of the poster.

  • 01 Center the live-performance image as the first focal point.
  • 02 Use high-contrast type for the artist, year, and tour identity.
  • 03 Keep the portrait format to frame the image and event details.

03 / The finished poster

One composition, moving from performer to tour title.

The final artwork pairs David Tao's live performance with the 2026 Soul Power World Tour identity. Contrast and scale guide the eye from the portrait to the event name in a single vertical layout.
